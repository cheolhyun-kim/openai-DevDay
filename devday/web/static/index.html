ABOUT ROTORSENSE

A clearer view of
rotating equipment.

RotorSense compares slow-motion video of industrial equipment with a registered healthy reference. It measures visible motion by component and frequency band to help teams spot changes worth inspecting.

Video-based screening supports inspection. It does not confirm a fault or replace calibrated vibration instruments.
